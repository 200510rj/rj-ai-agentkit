Company Paid Leave Policy:
Employees receive 18 annual paid leave days per calendar year.
Leave requests should normally be submitted through the internal HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
Emergency leave or sick leave should be communicated directly to your reporting manager via email or Slack.

Working Hours & Remote Work:
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time.
Employees are allowed up to 2 remote work days per week with manager approval.
